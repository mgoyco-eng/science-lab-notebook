Before you begin

How to Use This Platform

  • Complete all required Student Information fields first.
  • When the report unlocks, select Enter Focus Mode before you begin writing.
  • Write each section directly in the platform. Keep the current Science Guide provided by your teacher readily available and use it as your reference while you work.
  • Use Preview Report to check your report and data tables before the final download.
  • Click Download Final Report when instructed and make sure the PDF is on your device before leaving class.
!

Important

Copying, cutting, pasting, and dragging or dropping text are not permitted and are disabled on this page. Blocked attempts are recorded in Assessment Monitoring.

Your work is not saved. Refreshing or closing this page can permanently clear everything you typed. There is no Save Draft or Load Draft feature, so keep this page open until your final PDF has downloaded.
